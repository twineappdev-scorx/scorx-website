Training safety

Train with someone nearby

Train with a coach or adult nearby, warm up first, and stop if something hurts.

Goals you control, not leaderboards

Your results are compared with age and sex benchmarks and with your own past numbers. They're never ranked against your teammates. The goal line is yours to set, and the only person you need to beat is the you of last month.

Questions

Junior training app FAQ

Is the ScorX training app free?

Most of it is. Measure with 14 baseline metrics and age and sex benchmarking, goals, your Development Plan, the Drill and Play Libraries, Training Sessions and the Beep Test and time trials are all free. AI training recommendations and the full run drill library are ScorX Premium (A$4.99 a month or A$49.99 a year), with a 30-day free trial and no card needed.

What fitness tests can I log in ScorX?

Measure has 14 baseline metrics across speed, power, endurance and strength, such as the 10m and 40m sprints, standing jump height, the 2km run, max push-ups and max squat. You can add one custom metric for free, or unlimited custom metrics with ScorX Premium. Results are compared with age and sex benchmarks.

What does Premium add to Train?

Two things: AI training recommendations (and AI athleticism insight), and the full run drill library with sprint and interval drills plus custom run drills. The Beep Test, 800m and 2km time trials stay free. AI recommendations regenerate every 30 days or after 5 games.

Which sports have drills?

The Drill Library and Drill Animator have AFL drills, and there are running drills for every sport. The Play Library and Play Animator let you build and watch plays.

Is it safe for junior athletes to train with the app?

ScorX gives you drills, plans and tests, but it doesn't replace a coach. Train with a coach or adult nearby, warm up first, and stop if something hurts.
