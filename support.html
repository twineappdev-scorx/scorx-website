Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I download ScorX?

Download ScorX for free from the App Store. Search "ScorX" or visit our Download page. Requires iOS 18.0 or later.

Is ScorX really free?

Yes. ScorX Free is free forever, with no credit card required. It covers scoring, tracking and training tools for athletes, coaches and parents alike, with limits of 5 teams, 25 players and 20 saved games. Premium removes the limits and adds AI training.

How do I create my first team?

After downloading, complete the onboarding flow, select your sport, then tap "Create Team." Add your teammates' names, jersey numbers, and photos. Setup takes under 2 minutes.

Can I track multiple sports?

Yes. ScorX supports 16 sports, so you can track every sport you play. Switch between sports anytime from the home screen.

Subscriptions & Billing

What happens when I reach 20 saved games?

Free keeps up to 20 saved games. When you reach 20, delete a game or upgrade to Premium to save more. The 20 is a total, not a monthly limit.

What does ScorX Premium include?

ScorX Premium (A$49.99/year or A$4.99/month, 30-day free trial) removes the Free limits, with unlimited teams, players, saved games and custom metrics. It adds AI training recommendations and AI Season Narratives, voice stat recording (iOS 26 and later), the full run drill library, the full Player Card library with sticker format and card configurator, PDF & CSV export and iCloud sync across devices. Apple Family Sharing covers up to 5 members, so your parents can follow along.

How does the 30-day free trial work?

Download ScorX and tap "Start Free Trial" in the app. No credit card required. You get full Premium for 30 days, with the Free limits lifted. After 30 days, choose to subscribe or continue on ScorX Free.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → ScorX → Cancel Subscription. No cancellation fees.

Can my family follow along with my Premium subscription?

Yes. ScorX Premium supports Apple Family Sharing — share it with up to 5 family members at no extra cost, so your parents can follow along too.

What happens if I cancel ScorX Premium?

You'll continue with ScorX Free and lose access to Premium features (AI recommendations, voice recording, the full run drill library, the full Player Card library, iCloud sync and export). The Free limits of 5 teams, 25 players and 20 saved games apply again. Your data stays on your device.

Using ScorX

How do I start a new game?

Tap "New Game" from the home screen, select your sport and teams, configure timer settings, then tap "Start Game."

Can I use ScorX offline?

Yes. ScorX works fully offline. Score games anywhere without internet connection. iCloud sync occurs automatically when you reconnect (ScorX Premium only).

How do I record my own stats?

During a game, tap your name, then tap the action button (e.g., "Goal," "Assist," "Tackle"). The action is recorded instantly. Playing yourself? Score it after the game, or have a parent or coach tap it in from the sideline.

How do I export my game stats?

(ScorX Premium only) Open a completed game, tap the export icon, choose CSV or PDF format, then share via email or save to Files.

Sports & Features

Which sports does ScorX support?

ScorX supports 16 sports: AFL, Basketball, Netball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Touch Rugby, Soccer, Field Hockey, Volleyball, Water Polo, Tennis, Badminton, Squash, Racquetball, Pickleball, and Table Tennis.

Can you add [my sport]?

We're always expanding! Email your sport request to scorxapp@gmail.com and we'll consider it for future updates.

Does ScorX work for doubles in racquet sports?

Yes. ScorX supports singles and doubles for all racquet sports (Tennis, Badminton, Squash, Table Tennis, etc.).

What is the Post-Game Triage Dashboard?

After each game, you answer a short self-reflection on what went well and what to improve. If your team uses ScorX, the Triage Dashboard pulls everyone's reflections together and ranks the most common struggle areas, ready for the next training session. Available in ScorX Free.

What are Match Invitations and how do they work?

Match Invitations let your coach notify each player's family before game day. You or a parent accept the invite, confirm attendance, and record your stats during the game, while the coach sees an aggregated team view in real time. Available in ScorX Free, and nobody has to track every player alone.

How do AI Training Recommendations work?

ScorX Premium looks at your game statistics to find what to work on next and builds a training focus around it. Your recommendations refresh every 30 days or after 5 new games, so your training stays matched to what is actually happening in your games.

What is the Career Timeline?

The Career Timeline is a permanent visual record of your performance across every sport and season you play. It is included in ScorX Free, no Premium subscription required.

iCloud & Sync

How does iCloud sync work?

(ScorX Premium only) ScorX automatically syncs your teams, players, and game history across all devices signed into the same iCloud account. iCloud sync is not available on ScorX Free.

Why isn't my data syncing?

Check: (1) You have an active ScorX Premium subscription, (2) iCloud is enabled in Settings → ScorX → iCloud Sync, (3) You're signed into the same iCloud account on all devices, (4) You have an internet connection.

Is my data backed up?

(ScorX Premium) Yes, automatically via iCloud. (ScorX Free) Data is stored locally on your device — back up your iPhone via iCloud or iTunes to protect your records.

Privacy & Data

Does ScorX track my personal data?

No. ScorX has no third-party analytics, no ads, and no data sharing. Your game data stays on your device (or in your private iCloud if syncing).

Where is my data stored?

(ScorX Free) Locally on your iPhone/iPad in an encrypted database. (ScorX Premium) Locally + synced to your private iCloud account using Apple's end-to-end encryption.

Troubleshooting

The app crashed / froze. What do I do?

Force close the app and reopen. If the issue persists, email scorxapp@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.

How do I report a bug?

Email scorxapp@gmail.com with: (1) Description of the bug, (2) Steps to reproduce, (3) Device model, (4) iOS version. We'll investigate ASAP.
