Getting Started

How do I download ScorX?

ScorX is available on the App Store for iOS 18.0 or later. Simply search for "ScorX" in the App Store or click here to download.

What sports does ScorX support?

ScorX currently supports 9 sports:

  • AFL (Australian Football League)
  • Basketball
  • Netball
  • Rugby Union
  • Rugby League
  • Touch Rugby
  • Field Hockey
  • Soccer
  • Tennis

All sports are available on all subscription tiers, including the free plan.

Coming Soon: Water Polo, Badminton, and Table Tennis. Apple Watch integration for on-court scoring is planned for v1.2.

How do I create my first game?

After downloading ScorX:

  1. Complete the onboarding process and select your sport
  2. Create your team and add at least one player
  3. Tap "New Game" from the Home screen
  4. Select your team, opponent, and game details
  5. Start tracking! Tap activities to record scoring and statistics

Subscriptions & Pricing

How do I start my 30-day free trial?

Download ScorX and tap "Start Free Trial" on the Family plan during onboarding or in Settings → Subscriptions. The trial lasts 30 days and automatically converts to paid unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

What's the difference between Free and Family tiers?

Free Tier:

  • 1 Team, 1 Player, 5 Games/month
  • Local storage only (no cloud sync)

Family Tier ($4.99/month or $49.99/year):

  • 1 Team, up to 5 Players, 50 Games/month
  • iCloud Sync across all your devices
  • Data Export (CSV format)
  • 2 Years of historical data
  • Family Sharing (up to 5 family members)

Can I share my subscription with family members?

Yes! Family tier subscriptions support Apple Family Sharing, allowing you to share with up to 5 family members at no extra cost. Set up Family Sharing in your iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open iPhone Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Select "ScorX"
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period. You can also cancel from ScorX app Settings → Subscriptions → Manage.

When will Coach and Club tiers be available?

Advanced tiers with analytics, graphics generation, and multi-team management are planned for version 1.1, approximately 2-3 months after initial launch. Family plan subscribers will receive upgrade pricing notifications when these tiers become available.

Features & Functionality

Can I use ScorX offline?

Yes! ScorX works fully offline. All core features are available without an internet connection. If you're on the Family tier, your data will automatically sync to iCloud when you reconnect to the internet.

How do I sync my data across devices?

Data sync requires a Family tier subscription. Once subscribed:

  1. Ensure iCloud is enabled on all devices (Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud)
  2. Sign in with the same Apple ID on all devices
  3. Open ScorX on each device
  4. Data automatically syncs in the background

You'll see a sync status indicator in the app when syncing is in progress.

How do I export my data?

Family tier subscribers can export data:

  1. Go to Settings → Data Export
  2. Choose CSV format
  3. Select which games and data to export
  4. Tap "Export" and choose where to save or share

Note: PDF export will be available in the Coach tier (v1.1).

How do I switch between sports?

You can switch sports at any time:

  1. Go to Settings → Sport Selection
  2. Choose your new sport
  3. ScorX will intelligently migrate compatible data

Your existing games and players will remain accessible, and you can switch back anytime.

What statistics does ScorX track?

ScorX tracks sport-specific statistics:

  • AFL: Goals, behinds, disposals, marks, tackles, possessions
  • Basketball: Field goals, 3-pointers, free throws, rebounds, assists, steals, turnovers, fouls
  • Netball: Goals, centers, turnovers, intercepts, penalties
  • Rugby Union: Tries, conversions, penalties, drop goals, tackles
  • Rugby League: Tries, goals, tackles, meters gained
  • Touch Rugby: Tries, touches, rollballs, dummy half plays

Privacy & Security

Is my data private?

Absolutely. ScorX is built with privacy as a core principle:

  • No third-party analytics or tracking
  • No advertisements
  • No data selling or sharing
  • Local data encrypted with SQLite
  • Cloud data protected by Apple's iCloud end-to-end encryption

Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete data in several ways:

  • Individual games: Swipe left on any game in your history and tap Delete
  • Teams/Players: Tap Edit in team or player management, then Delete
  • All local data: Uninstall the app
  • CloudKit data: Contact support@scorx.app for complete deletion (processed within 7 business days)

Technical Support

What iOS version do I need?

ScorX requires iOS 18.0 or later. It works on iPhone and iPad.

I'm having sync issues. What should I do?

If you're experiencing sync problems:

  1. Check that you're signed into iCloud (Settings → [Your Name])
  2. Ensure iCloud Drive is enabled
  3. Check your internet connection
  4. Force quit and reopen ScorX
  5. Try toggling iCloud sync off and on in ScorX Settings

If issues persist, contact support@scorx.app with details about your device and iOS version.

The app crashed. How do I report a bug?

We apologize for any crashes. Please email support@scorx.app with:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version
  • ScorX app version (found in Settings → About)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • What you were doing when it crashed

This helps us fix bugs quickly!
