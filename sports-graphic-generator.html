What each one actually looks like

Everything lives under Share Cards, one tap from the game you just finished.

One menu, every graphic

Share Cards is the hub. Set your team logo once and add the opposition's per game, then choose what to make: the result, the team's numbers, or a single player's game.

  • Team Logos — your club crest, saved once in Team Management, on every graphic after that
  • Game Results — Final Score Cards in Broadcast or Split Rail
  • Stats Overview — Team, Attacking, Midfield and Defence, each as a table or a chart
  • Players — a stat card for any player on the sheet

Final score graphics

ScorX Free

The result, in the format the sport actually uses. AFL shows 27 with the 4.3 underneath. Netball, rugby and soccer each show their own breakdown.

  • Broadcast — big centred scoreline with both crests, built for a feed post
  • Split Rail — the alternative layout when you want the photo to carry the image
  • Add Game Photo — drop a shot from the day in behind the score
  • Show Score Type Count — toggle the goals/behinds style breakdown on or off
  • Competition and round labelled automatically from the game you set up

Player stat graphics

Choose the player, choose which stats go on the card, and ScorX builds it. Every number comes from what was recorded live — nothing is typed in twice.

  • Pick the stats yourself, grouped by category, with slot ordering so the big number leads
  • Card types — Scouting Report, Full Time, Holographic, Player of the Match and more
  • Styles — Frosted, Dark or Light; Compact or Standard size
  • ScorX or Team Colors — run the card in your own club's palette
  • Optional player photo, game photo, and physical measurements (age, height, weight, wingspan) in SI or Imperial

One standard card style is free for everyone. The full style library, sticker format and custom colour themes are ScorX Premium.

Team stat graphics

Pick the stats that tell the story

A card with twelve numbers on it says nothing. ScorX lets you choose the four or five that actually made the game, per player, per card.

  • Stats grouped the way the sport groups them — scoring, midfield, defensive
  • Slot ordering decides what leads the card
  • Player name, number, position and team pulled from the roster
  • Toggle a game photo behind the card in one tap

Sports graphic generator FAQ

What is a sports graphic generator?

A sports graphic generator turns match data into a finished, shareable image — a score update, a final score card, or a player's stat line — without design software. ScorX generates these automatically from the game you scored in the app, so the numbers on the graphic are the numbers you recorded.

Do I have to type the scores in to make a graphic?

No. ScorX graphics are built from the game you scored live in the app. Score the match on your phone and every graphic — final score, player stats, team stat charts — is already populated. There is no separate data entry step and no template to fill in.

What sports graphics can ScorX make?

Final Score Cards in Broadcast and Split Rail layouts, live score update graphics during a match, player stat cards in several card types including Scouting Report, Full Time, Holographic and Player of the Match, and team stat graphics as either a Stats Table or a Stats Chart — available for team totals and for attacking, midfield and defensive categories. The library grows with each app update.

Are ScorX sports graphics free?

Yes. Score graphics, team stat graphics and one standard Player Card style are included in ScorX Free, with no limit on how many you make. ScorX Premium (A$4.99/month or A$49.99/year) unlocks the full Player Card style library, sticker format cards, and custom colour themes with the full card configurator.

Can I add my club's logo and colours to the graphics?

Yes. Your team logo is set once in Team Management and appears on every graphic after that. You can add an opposition logo per game, switch player cards between ScorX branding and your own team colours, and drop a photo from the game in behind the score.

Do the graphics have a ScorX watermark?

Every card carries a small ScorX mark discreetly in the corner. It is the same mark on ScorX Free and on ScorX Premium — subscribing does not remove it, and there is no paid “remove branding” option. Aside from that mark the graphic is entirely yours: your team logo, your colours, your photo, and no ads or promotional text.

Where can I post the graphics?

Anywhere. Each graphic can be saved to your camera roll or shared straight from ScorX via the iOS share sheet — Instagram Stories, Facebook, WhatsApp, the team group chat, or a club newsletter. The images are sized for social posts and stories.

Does the graphic generator work offline?

Yes. Graphics are rendered on your device, so you can score a game, generate a final score card and save it with no reception at the ground. Sharing happens whenever you next have a connection.

Which sports are supported?

All 16 sports ScorX supports. Graphics use each sport's own scoring format and stat categories — AFL cards show goals, behinds and the composite score, netball shows the shooting split, rugby shows tries and conversions, and so on. See the full sports list.

Will new graphic formats be added?

Yes — the format library is growing. New card types and layouts ship with app updates and show up automatically under Share Cards, at no extra cost on whichever plan you're on. If there's a format your club needs, email scorxapp@gmail.com and tell us what it should show.
