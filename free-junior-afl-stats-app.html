“Free” means three different things in footy apps

It's worth knowing which one you're downloading before round one.

1

Free until you hit the cap

The most common shape: free to download, then limited to one team, one player, or a handful of games. It works beautifully for a month and runs out somewhere around the mid-season bye.

2

Free for now

Launch pricing, described as free for a limited time. Genuinely free today, with no commitment about what it costs once your season's data is already in it.

3

Free if your club pays

Free to you because the club bought a licence. Fine while you're at that club — less useful when your kid changes clubs, or when the committee changes its mind.

ScorX Free is none of those three.

No cap, no countdown, no club gatekeeping, no card. If you never pay us a cent, you keep unlimited scoring, tracking and history for as long as you use the app.
