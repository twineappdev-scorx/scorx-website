The tournament ends. Did any of it stick?

Every major tournament, football grounds everywhere fill up with kids trying the tricks they just watched on a screen.

Then the tournament ends, and most families have nothing to show whether any of it stuck. Plenty of apps handle fixtures and results. A separate, more aggressive category of apps handles recruiting profiles and pathway talk. Almost nothing quietly tracks the actual skills underneath — the first touch, the tackle, the pass that actually got through.

What this isn't: ScorX doesn't rank players, generate exposure, or feed a recruiting pipeline. It's for the question every kid actually asks after watching their team — can I do that too, and am I getting better at it?
