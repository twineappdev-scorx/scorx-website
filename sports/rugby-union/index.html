Built for community rugby.

Rugby development has always run on feel.

Selectors talk about a player who "reads the game well" or "has a good engine." Coaches know who's improved their tackle technique since March. None of it gets written down, and by the end of a season, the only thing anyone really remembers is whether the team won.

Mini rugby parents watching their first contact season have even less to go on — no scoreboard most weeks, no stats, just a sideline full of effort and very little record of it.

ScorX gives rugby what selectors have always talked about but never measured.

The skills that make up a rugby performance

Rugby Union has more distinct skill areas than most community sports — because the game itself does. ScorX tracks them properly, including the parts of the game that are specific to union and easy for generic stats apps to miss entirely.

💪

Tackling

Getting low, getting front-on, finishing the contact.

🤝

Passing

Under pressure, both hands, both directions.

🦵

Kicking

Territory, restarts, contestable kicks.

🏉

Ball Carrying

Metres gained, contact won, offloads.

🔄

Rucking / Support

Arriving, securing, recycling. The part of the game that never makes the highlight reel and decides most matches.

🛡️

Defensive Line

Staying connected, reading the attack before it commits.

🗣️

Communication

Calling moves, organising the line, talking through structure.

⚡

Effort / Intensity

The unglamorous work rate that every good coach actually values most.

Rucking and support play exist as their own tracked category because that's how union actually works — most generic stats tools either ignore it or fold it into something else, which misses most of what happens in a real match.
