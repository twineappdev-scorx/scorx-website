ScorX is a free iOS app that keeps time, score, and activity records — goals, intercepts and centre passes — for youth netball. Celebrate success with shareable graphics and develop performance with training modules.

See her position development — free

Built in Australia for community netball. Developing players, not ranking them.

Netball seasons run on position trials, and most of it happens invisibly.

A coach moves your daughter from GA to WA in round 6 and you find out at quarter time. Maybe it's about footwork. Maybe it's about decision-making under the third-second rule. Maybe it's just that someone else was free that week. You genuinely don't know — and asking can feel like the kind of question that makes you "that parent."

Coaches aren't hiding anything. They just don't have an easy way to show the reasoning, especially mid-season with eight players, four positions, and one training night a week to work it all out.

ScorX puts the data next to the decision, for everyone.

The skills behind every position

Netball has more individually tracked actions than almost any other community sport — footwork, passing accuracy, interceptions, all happening in tight spaces with strict rules most other sports don't have. ScorX tracks it properly.
