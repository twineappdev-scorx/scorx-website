Built in Australia for community footy. No ladders, no rankings, no recruiting.

Junior footy is full of effort and almost no evidence.

You train twice a week and play every Saturday, and by round 15 it's hard to say exactly what's improved. The AFL and AFLW have Champion Data. Junior footy has a scoreboard and a memory that fades by Tuesday.

Coaches feel it too. Your coach can see you tackling better than you were in March, but there's been nothing to show it. Now there's a record, so a move off half-forward comes with a reason.

ScorX gives junior footy what the big league has always had: a real record.
