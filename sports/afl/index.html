What gets reflected back to your child

Not every stat needs a number next to it. ScorX surfaces the eight things that actually move the needle in junior AFL development.

🦵

Kicking

Every time the ball leaves the boot, on target or not.

🙌

Marking

Clean hands under pressure, contested or uncontested.

💪

Tackling

Getting front-on and finishing it.

👊

Handball

Quick hands out of trouble.

🏉

Disposal

Every touch, whether kicked or handballed, that keeps it your team's footy.

🏃

Running / Movement

The unseen stuff: getting to the contest first.

🧭

Positioning

Being where the play needs you, not just where the ball is.

🗣️

Communication

Calling for it, organising the defensive line.
