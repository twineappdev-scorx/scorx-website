The tournament ends. Did the inspiration turn into improvement?

Every four years, a World Cup happens and every junior pitch in the country fills up with kids trying out the moves they just watched on TV.

Then the tournament ends, the posters come down, and most families have no record of whether any of that inspiration actually turned into improvement. There are plenty of apps for schedules and scores. There's a separate, more aggressive category of apps built around recruiting profiles and exposure. Almost nothing sits in between, quietly tracking the actual skills a kid is building.

A note on what this isn't: ScorX doesn't rank players, generate exposure, or feed a recruiting pipeline. It's for the question every kid actually has after watching their favourite team — am I getting better at this?

The skills behind every match, not just the score

⚽

Shooting

Every attempt, on target or not, the moment that matters most to a young player's confidence.

🤝

Passing

Accuracy and decision-making, not just how many got through.

⚡

Dribbling

Close control and the ability to beat a defender one-on-one.

🛡️

Tackling

Winning the ball back cleanly. Coachable, and rarely tracked anywhere else.

✋

First Touch

Receiving the ball under pressure. Arguably the single most telling technical skill at junior level, and the one most stat sheets skip entirely.

🧭

Positioning

Defensive shape, attacking runs, being where the structure needs a player.

🗣️

Communication

Calling for it, organising the press.

🔥

Effort / Intensity

Universal, and the first thing every coach actually watches for.

Goals and assists are outcomes — they're visible on the scoreboard already. ScorX tracks the skills underneath them, the ones that actually decide whether a kid is improving.
