Built for community basketball. We're not a recruiting platform — we're for kids who want to know they're getting better.

Am I getting better?

Rec league and travel ball stat sheets are inconsistent at best, and often don't exist at all.

Parents already track numbers in their heads, or scribble them on the back of a program. Players want to know if their shot is actually falling more often than last month, not just whether the team won. And in a culture full of mixtapes, rankings and exposure camps, almost nothing actually answers the simple question: am I getting better?

That's the only question ScorX is built to answer.

A note on what this isn't: ScorX doesn't rank players, generate exposure, or feed a recruiting pipeline. There are already plenty of apps trying to be your kid's ticket to somewhere else. This one is for actually getting better at the game in front of them.

Points scored and turnovers are outcomes, not skills — they're visible already. ScorX focuses on the things underneath the box score, because that's where real development actually happens.
