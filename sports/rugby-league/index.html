Tackles made. Metres gained. Every carry counted.

ScorX tracks junior league the way the game's actually played — fast, physical, repetition after repetition — so the effort on the field finally shows up somewhere.

By full time, all anyone remembers is the score.

You're on the hill. Your kid's made a dozen tackles, carried it up the middle three times, played the ball quick out of dummy half. By full time, all anyone remembers is the score.

League rewards effort and repetition more than almost any other junior sport — and almost none of it gets recorded. Coaches know who's tackling better than round one. They just can't show it.

ScorX counts what league actually runs on.

The skills that make up a league performance

League moves fast, and the things that matter — tackle efficiency, quick play-the-balls, metres gained — usually disappear the moment the next set starts. ScorX tracks them properly.
