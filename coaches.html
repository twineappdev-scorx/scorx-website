Coaches FAQ

Is ScorX really free for coaches?

Yes — completely free, with no artificial limits. Coaches get unlimited teams, unlimited roster sizes, and unlimited games per month at no cost and with no credit card required. The free experience is designed to be genuinely useful across an entire season.

What are Match Invitations and how do they work?

Match Invitations let coaches send a pre-game notification directly to each player's family before game day. Parents accept the invite, confirm attendance, and are set up to record their child's stats during the game. The coach sees the full team stat view in real time. It turns a single-person chore into a team effort.

How does distributed stat recording work?

When a coach sends match invitations, each parent records their own child's stats from within the app during the game. The coach sees an aggregated team view updated in real time. This removes the bottleneck of one person trying to track 15 players simultaneously.

What is the Post-Game Triage Dashboard?

After each game, players submit a structured self-reflection covering what went well and what to improve. The Triage Dashboard aggregates these submissions across the whole roster and surfaces the most common struggle areas as a ranked list — ready for your next training session. It also shows how many players have submitted so you can prompt stragglers.

What does Perception vs Reality analysis show me?

Perception vs Reality compares each player's self-assessed performance against their actual recorded statistics. It surfaces overconfidence (a player thinks they played well but the data disagrees), underconfidence (a player undervalues a genuine strength), and coaching blind spots. It makes development conversations evidence-based instead of opinion-based.

How many teams and players can I manage?

Unlimited. There is no cap on the number of teams, roster size, or games per month on the free tier. A coach running multiple teams across several sports can manage everything in a single app without needing to upgrade.

What does ScorX Premium add for coaches?

ScorX Premium unlocks AI Training Recommendations — a weekly coaching focus generated directly from your team's match statistics and player self-assessments. It identifies the specific areas where your team struggled last week and suggests targeted training priorities, not generic drills. Premium also unlocks the full athlete development suite for players on your roster: the Train section, athleticism benchmarking, Player Card library, and Career Timeline. At $4.99/month or $49.99/year, it's for coaches who want every opportunity to grow their team.
