Coaches FAQ

How many teams and players can I manage with the Coach tier?

The Coach tier supports up to 5 teams and 150 players total, with 100 games per month. You can switch between teams seamlessly and view aggregated statistics across your entire coaching programme.

What is the Team Triage Dashboard?

Team Triage aggregates player self-assessments across your entire roster and shows the most common development focus areas as a team. This allows you to identify where the majority of your players need to improve and plan training sessions that address the whole group—not just individual outliers.

How do AI Team Training Plans work for coaches?

ScorX's AI analyses your team's aggregated game statistics and player self-assessments to generate a recommended training plan for the upcoming week. Plans are grounded in real performance data, targeting the specific skills and tactics your team's collective results indicate need the most work.

What is the perception versus reality comparison?

ScorX compares each player's self-assessed strengths and weaknesses against their actual game statistics. This Advanced Analytics feature shows coaches where players have accurate self-awareness versus where they over- or under-estimate their own performance—valuable information for targeted conversations and development planning.

Can I export professional reports to share with parents or the club?

Yes. The Coach tier includes PDF export with custom templates and Professional Player Cards. These can be shared with parents, players, or club administrators to communicate individual and team development progress in a polished, professional format.

Does ScorX work for coaching multiple sports?

Yes. ScorX supports 16 sports and each team can be configured for a different sport. If you coach across multiple disciplines—for example AFL in winter and basketball in summer—you can manage all teams and their sport-specific statistics within a single app.
