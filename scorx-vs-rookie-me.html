ScorX vs Rookie Me Play: which junior footy app is right for you?

If you're a coach or club running rotations and game-time compliance across a team, Rookie Me Play is built for that. If you're a parent or player who wants a record of one athlete's games, stats and progress across seasons, ScorX is built for that. Plenty of families use both.

What Rookie Me Play does well

  • Rotations and interchange: planning who is on the ground and when is its core job.
  • Game-time compliance: tracking game time against junior rules across a team.
  • Synced data collection: several people can record a game across devices.
  • League-wide oversight: Rookie Me Hub gives clubs and leagues an admin view.

What ScorX does differently

  • One athlete in focus: the record belongs to the player, not the team sheet.
  • Multi-season timeline: games, stats and progress build up season after season.
  • Training from game data: the free Train tools include a Development Plan and drill library, with AI recommendations on Premium.
  • Highlight cards: turn a game into a shareable player card.
  • Privacy: no ads. We never sell your data. Data collection is opt-in and only used to improve the app.

Which should you choose?

Using both

The two apps suit different people at the same game. The club runs Rookie Me Play for rotations and game time, and the parent runs ScorX for their own child's record. Nothing needs to be linked. If you coach as well, ScorX has free coaching tools, and rotations and time played on iPad.

Choose Rookie Me Play if…

  • You run a team, club or league and need rotations and game time managed
  • You need an admin portal for several teams
  • Checking game time against junior rules is your main job

Choose ScorX if…

  • You want one player's stats, progress and season history in one place
  • You want free training tools and shareable player cards
  • You want to start free with no ads and no card
