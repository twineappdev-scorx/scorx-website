Pricing FAQ

Is there really a free tier forever?

Yes! ScorX Free is always free with 20 games per month across all 16 sports. No credit card required, ever.

What happens after my 30-day Family trial ends?

You can choose to subscribe to Family tier ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) or continue using ScorX Free. Your game data and statistics are preserved.

What's included in AI Training Recommendations?

ScorX analyses your game statistics to identify skill development priorities, athleticism gaps, and training focus areas. Recommendations regenerate every 30 days or after 5 new games. Available on Family tier and above.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings. No cancellation fees.

What is Family Sharing?

Family and Coach tier subscriptions can be shared with up to 5 family members through Apple Family Sharing at no additional cost.

What does the Coach tier add beyond Family?

The Coach tier adds Team Triage Dashboard (aggregated player assessments), AI Team Training Plans, Advanced Analytics (perception vs reality comparison), Professional Player Cards, PDF Export, and Multi-Team Management (up to 5 teams, 150 players).

Do subscriptions auto-renew?

Yes, through the App Store. You can turn off auto-renewal anytime in your Apple ID settings.
