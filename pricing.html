What's the difference?

ScorX Free runs your team. ScorX Premium develops your athlete.

ScorX Free is for coaches who want to run a team efficiently.

The free tier creates investment. A coach records games. Parents contribute stats via Match Invitations. Players submit reflections. Game data accumulates across a season. The post-game Triage Dashboard and Perception vs Reality analysis already provide meaningful coaching intelligence — all at zero cost.

ScorX Premium is for parents who want to turn that data into action.

Premium rewards the investment of recording. It takes the raw statistics and reflections and turns them into personalised AI training guidance, physical benchmarking against peers, shareable Player Cards, a career-spanning timeline, and season narratives — the development story of a sporting life.

Pricing FAQ

Is ScorX Free really unlimited?

Yes. ScorX Free has no caps on teams, players, or games. A coach running multiple teams across a full season will never hit a limit. The free tier is designed to be genuinely useful — not a crippled trial designed to push you to pay.

What does ScorX Premium add?

ScorX Premium (A$4.99/month or A$49.99/year AUD) adds the athlete development intelligence layer: AI-powered training recommendations from the Train section, age and sex-specific athleticism benchmarking, the full Player Card style library, the Career Timeline, AI-generated Season Narratives, and exportable PDF reports.

Is there a free trial for ScorX Premium?

Yes — ScorX Premium includes a 30-day free trial. No credit card is required to start. After 30 days you can subscribe or continue using ScorX Free with no loss of recorded data.

What happened to the old Family and Coach tiers?

As of April 2026 the tier model has been simplified. ScorX Free replaces the old Free tier with genuinely unlimited capabilities. ScorX Premium replaces the Family and Coach tiers as a single development intelligence subscription. All coaching tools are now free with no exceptions.

Can I cancel ScorX Premium anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings with no cancellation fees. Your game data and recorded statistics are preserved on your device regardless of subscription status.

Does ScorX Premium support Family Sharing?

Yes. ScorX Premium can be shared with up to 5 family members through Apple Family Sharing at no additional cost. One subscription covers the whole family.
