Pricing FAQ

What does ScorX Free include, and what are the limits?

ScorX Free covers live scoring in 16 sports, your game history, 14 athleticism metrics with age and sex benchmarking, your Development Plan, drills, plays and training sessions. The limits are 5 teams, 25 players in total and 20 saved games. Premium removes them. Playing footy? See what the free junior AFL stats app includes.

What happens when I reach 20 saved games?

Free keeps up to 20 saved games. When you reach 20, delete a game or upgrade to Premium to save more. The 20 is a total, not a monthly limit.

What does ScorX Premium add?

ScorX Premium (A$4.99/month or A$49.99/year AUD) removes the limits, with unlimited teams, players, saved games and custom metrics. It adds AI training recommendations, AI Season Narratives, voice stat recording on iOS 26 and later, the full run drill library, the full Player Card library, PDF and CSV export and iCloud sync.

Is there a free trial for ScorX Premium?

Yes. Try full Premium free for 30 days, no card required. Every Premium feature is on and the Free limits are lifted. After 30 days you can subscribe or carry on with ScorX Free. If your trial ends or you stop Premium while you're over the Free limits, everything you've recorded is kept and you can still view it. You can't add new games, teams or players until you're back under the limit or upgrade.

Do I have to pay to record my games and see my stats?

No. Scoring, your game history (up to 20 saved games), the 14 athleticism metrics with trend indicators and benchmarking, the Career Timeline, your Development Plan and training sessions are all in ScorX Free. Premium is for when you want AI training recommendations, the full Player Card library, exports and no limits.

Can I cancel ScorX Premium anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime in your App Store settings, with no cancellation fees. If your trial ends or you stop Premium while you're over the Free limits, everything you've recorded is kept and you can still view it. You can't add new games, teams or players until you're back under the limit or upgrade.

Does ScorX Premium support Family Sharing?

Yes. ScorX Premium can be shared with up to 5 family members through Apple Family Sharing at no additional cost, so your parents can follow along too. One subscription covers the whole family.
