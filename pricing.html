Pricing FAQ

Is there really a free tier forever?

Yes! ScorX Free is always free with 1 team, 1 player, and 5 games per month. No credit card required, ever.

What happens after my 30-day Family trial ends?

You can choose to subscribe to Family tier ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) or continue using ScorX Free. Teams, players, and games must be deleted to match the Free tier limits before new games can be recorded.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings. No cancellation fees.

What is Family Sharing?

Family tier subscriptions can be shared with up to 5 family members through Apple Family Sharing at no additional cost.

When will Coach and Club tiers launch?

Coach and Club tiers are planned for v1.1 (mid-2025). Email scorxapp@gmail.com to receive updates.

Do subscriptions auto-renew?

Yes, through the App Store. You can turn off auto-renewal anytime in your Apple ID settings.
