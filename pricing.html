Pricing FAQ

Is ScorX Basic really free forever?

Yes! ScorX Basic is always free with score tracking and game timer across all 16 sports. No credit card required, ever.

What happens after my 30-day ScorX Athlete trial ends?

You can choose to subscribe to ScorX Athlete ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) or continue using ScorX Basic. Your game data and statistics are preserved either way.

What is The Progress Report?

The Progress Report is your AI-generated monthly development summary — it shows what's improved, what to focus on, and how this season compares to last. It updates every 30 days or after 5 new games. Available on ScorX Athlete and above.

What is The Highlight Card?

After every game, ScorX generates a shareable stat card for each player — built for Instagram, designed to celebrate the moments that matter. Available on ScorX Athlete and above.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.

What is Family Sharing?

ScorX Athlete and Team subscriptions can be shared with up to 5 family members through Apple Family Sharing at no additional cost.

What does ScorX Team add beyond ScorX Athlete?

ScorX Team is built for coaches. It adds The Team Dashboard (squad-wide view), Player Development Reports (shareable with families), The Coaching Edge (AI weekly training focus), Distributed Recording (parents record their own child's stats), Perception vs Reality Insights, Season Certificates, and multi-team management for up to 5 teams and 150 players.

Do subscriptions auto-renew?

Yes, through the App Store. You can turn off auto-renewal anytime in your Apple ID settings.
