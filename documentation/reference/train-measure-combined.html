ScorX · Train + Measure · Combined view design

Training and measurement, one destination.

Train and Measure were two separate surfaces for the same job — developing a player. The combined view puts them under a single segment control: Train for goals, drills, and AI insights; Measure for entering and reviewing physical metrics. Less navigation, more focus.
