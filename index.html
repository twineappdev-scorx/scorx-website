Frequently Asked Questions

How do AI Training Recommendations work? +

The Progress Report analyses your game statistics to identify what's improved, what to focus on, and how this season compares to last. It regenerates monthly or after 5 new games, so it always reflects where your athlete is right now. Available on ScorX Athlete and above.

Will it work offline at remote grounds? +

Yes! ScorX has full offline functionality. Score games anywhere without internet connection. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

Is it complicated to set up? +

Not at all! The average user sets up their first team roster in under 2 minutes. Just add player names and you're ready to start scoring. ScorX is designed for parents and coaches on the go.

Can I try before I buy? +

Start with ScorX Basic (no credit card required) or enjoy a 30-day free trial of ScorX Athlete with full features including The Progress Report and The Highlight Card. Cancel anytime with one tap.

Can I export my data? +

Yes! All game statistics and player performance data can be exported to CSV format at any time. ScorX Team includes PDF export with custom templates. Your data belongs to you.

Which sports are supported? +

ScorX supports 16 sports: AFL, Basketball, Netball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Touch Rugby, Soccer, Field Hockey, Volleyball, Water Polo, Tennis, Badminton, Squash, Racquetball, Pickleball, and Table Tennis. Each sport has custom scoring rules, sport-specific statistics, and AI-powered development insights.
