Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start my 30-day free trial?

Download ScorX from the App Store, tap "Start Free Trial" on the Family plan during onboarding or in Subscription settings. Your trial automatically converts to paid unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Can I use ScorX offline?

Yes! ScorX works fully offline. All features are available without an internet connection. Cloud sync occurs automatically when you reconnect (Family tier and above).

What sports does ScorX support?

ScorX currently supports 9 sports: AFL, Basketball, Netball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Touch Rugby, Field Hockey, Soccer, and Tennis. All sports are available on all subscription tiers, including the free plan. Water Polo, Badminton, and Table Tennis are coming soon, with Apple Watch integration for on-court scoring planned for v1.2.

Can I share my subscription with family?

Yes! Family tier subscriptions support Apple Family Sharing, allowing you to share with up to 5 family members at no extra cost.

When will Coach and Club tiers be available?

Advanced tiers with analytics, graphics generation, and multi-team features are planned for version 1.1, approximately 2-3 months after initial launch. Family plan subscribers will receive upgrade pricing notifications.

How do I export my data?

Family tier subscribers can export game data and player statistics to CSV format from Settings → Data Export. PDF export will be available in the Coach tier (v1.1).
