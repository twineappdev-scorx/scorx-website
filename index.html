Frequently Asked Questions

Will it work offline at remote grounds? +

Yes! ScorX has full offline functionality. Score games anywhere without internet connection. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

Is it complicated to set up? +

Not at all! The average user sets up their first team roster in under 2 minutes. Just add player names and you're ready to start scoring.

What if I'm not tech-savvy? +

ScorX is designed with intuitive, easy-to-use interfaces. No training needed—if you can use your phone, you can use ScorX. We've specifically designed it for parents and coaches on the go.

Can I try before I buy? +

Absolutely! Start with the free tier (no credit card required) or enjoy a 30-day free trial of the Family tier with full features. Cancel anytime with one tap.

Can I export my data if I stop using ScorX? +

Yes! Your data exports with you. All game statistics and player performance data can be exported to CSV format at any time. Your memories belong to you.

Which sports are supported? +

ScorX supports 16 sports: AFL, Basketball, Netball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Touch Rugby, Soccer, Field Hockey, Volleyball, Water Polo, Tennis, Badminton, Squash, Racquetball, Pickleball, and Table Tennis. Each sport has custom scoring rules and 100+ statistical activities.
