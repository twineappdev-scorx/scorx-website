Fair question

"Isn't this just more pressure?"

Here's the honest answer.

Developing, not monitoring

ScorX isn't about being watched. It's your own record, there to help you get better. The data is for your development, and it's yours to share or keep.

Celebrating, not comparing

Cards measure you against one person: yourself last month. There are no leaderboards and no comparing with teammates, just your own progress, worth sharing.

You're already putting in the work

You haven't missed a training or a game all season. ScorX makes sure all that effort adds up to something you can look back on.
