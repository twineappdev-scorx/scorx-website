Youth Athlete Performance Tracking & AI Sports Training Recommendations

ScorX is the junior athlete development app that turns your game stats into training you can act on. Score it, track it, then train it. Score it yourself after the game, or have a parent or coach tap it in from the sideline.

Player Development FAQ

How does AI Training Intelligence work?

ScorX analyses your game statistics after each match to find your skill development priorities and training focus areas. Personalised training recommendations refresh every 30 days or after 5 new games, so your training stays aligned with what your game needs, not guesswork. AI training recommendations are part of ScorX Premium.

What is Athleticism Benchmarking?

Athleticism Benchmarking lets you log results across 14 baseline metrics, including sprint speed, vertical jump height and endurance, then compares them against age and sex-specific benchmarks. You can also add your own custom metrics: 1 on ScorX Free and unlimited on ScorX Premium. Benchmarking is free.

How many teams, players and games can I save on ScorX Free?

ScorX Free covers up to 5 teams, 25 players and 20 saved games in total. When you reach 20 games, delete a game or upgrade to ScorX Premium to save another. ScorX Premium has unlimited teams, players and saved games.

What does the 30-day trial include?

The trial is full ScorX Premium for 30 days with no card required. Every Premium feature is unlocked, with no team, player or game limits during the trial.

Can I customise which activities are tracked?

Yes. ScorX supports 100+ activities across all 16 sports and lets you choose which activities appear during live scoring. That way you capture the statistics most relevant to your own development priorities.

Does ScorX work without internet?

Yes. ScorX works fully offline, so you can score games anywhere, including remote grounds with no mobile coverage. iCloud sync across your devices is part of ScorX Premium and syncs automatically when your connection returns.

What is Player Sentiment Tracking?

Player Sentiment Tracking lets you record how you felt during a match: positive, neutral or negative. Over time it shows the patterns between your mood and your performance, so you, your coach and your family can support your wellbeing alongside your development.

What do I need for voice stat recording?

Voice stat recording is part of ScorX Premium. It works for team sports and needs iOS 26 or later. The rest of ScorX runs on iOS 18 or later.

Can I import my team from PlayHQ?

Yes. Export your team from PlayHQ as a spreadsheet (.xlsx or .csv) and import it into ScorX for free, so you do not have to retype the roster.
