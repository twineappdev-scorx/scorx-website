ScorX delivers professional-grade scoring, statistics, and analytics in a beautifully simple interface.

Real-Time Game Scoring

  • Record scores, track time, and capture every play as it happens
  • Sport-specific scoring rules ensure accuracy (goals, tries, points, sets)
  • Automatic score calculations and game flow management
  • Visual scoreboard updates in real-time

Works with all 14 sports

Comprehensive Player Statistics

  • Track individual player performance with 100+ customizable activities
  • AFL: Disposals, marks, tackles, goals, possessions
  • Basketball: Field goals, 3-pointers, rebounds, assists, steals
  • Tennis: Aces, double faults, winners, unforced errors
  • Netball, Rugby, Soccer, and all other supported sports

Visual Performance Analytics

  • Interactive charts showing player activity breakdown

  • Activity timelines showing when actions occurred during games

Configurable Game Timers

Easy Team & Roster Management

Complete Game History & Archives

  • Searchable game history with filters by sport, team, date
  • Review detailed statistics from any past game
