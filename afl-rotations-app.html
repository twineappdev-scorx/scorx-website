AFL Rotations App for iPad: Free Junior Footy Lineups

ScorX is an iPad app that helps junior AFL coaches plan lineups on a magnet board, run rotations from the bench and see time played for every player. It is free for coaches.

Download Free

What ScorX does on game day

Everything here is free, on iPad.

Magnet board lineups

Drag player tokens onto the ground, set your starting side and plan the interchange before the first bounce.

Rotations

Record interchanges as they happen, with the reason: injury, rest, card or tactical.

Time played per player

See how long every player has been on the ground, so the quiet kid on the bench doesn't get forgotten.

Live scoring

Score the game and record stats on the same iPad, fully offline. Parents can tap in stats too with Match Invitations.

What it doesn't do yet

  • No automatic Junior Rules check. The national AFL Junior Rules say every player must play a minimum of 50-75% of game time, with the controlling body (your league or competition) setting the exact percentage and injured players excluded. ScorX shows time played, but you compare it with your league's figure yourself.
  • No iPhone rotations. Rotations, time played and the magnet board are iPad only.
  • No club admin portal. ScorX works at team level, for you and your families.

How it fits with club tools like Rookie Me Play

If your club runs Rookie Me Play, keep using it for game-time compliance and club administration. It is built for that. ScorX does a different job: it keeps a development record for each player, with their stats, progress, training and season history, and gives the coach a free lineup and rotation board on iPad.

Plenty of clubs and families use both. For the full picture, read ScorX vs Rookie Me Play.

Pricing: all coaching tools are free

ScorX Free covers up to 5 teams, 25 players in total and 20 saved games, with lineups, rotations, time played and live scoring included. No credit card. ScorX Premium is A$4.99 a month or A$49.99 a year and removes the limits, with a 30-day free trial and no card required. See pricing.
