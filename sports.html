16 Sports. Unlimited Possibilities.

From the footy field to the tennis court, ScorX adapts to your sport with custom scoring rules and activity tracking.

Team Sports

Professional scorekeeping for team-based sports with multi-player tracking, team statistics, and comprehensive activity logging.

🏈

AFL (Australian Football)

Scoring: Goals (6 pts), Behinds (1 pt)

Activities: Disposals, Marks, Tackles, Kicks, Handballs, Possessions

Timer: 4 quarters (20 minutes each)

🏀

Basketball

Scoring: Field Goals (2 pts), 3-Pointers (3 pts), Free Throws (1 pt)

Activities: Rebounds, Assists, Steals, Blocks, Turnovers

Timer: 4 quarters (configurable)

🏐

Netball

Scoring: Goals (1 pt each)

Activities: Goals, Centers, Turnovers, Intercepts, Penalties

Timer: 4 quarters (15 minutes each)

🏉

Rugby Union

Scoring: Tries (5 pts), Conversions (2 pts), Penalties (3 pts), Drop Goals (2 pts)

Activities: Tackles, Meters Gained, Lineouts, Scrums, Rucks

Timer: 2 halves (40 minutes each)

🏉

Rugby League

Scoring: Tries (4 pts), Conversions (2 pts), Penalties (2 pts)

Activities: Tackles, Meters Gained, Tackle Breaks, Offloads

Timer: 2 halves (40 minutes each)

👐

Touch Rugby

Scoring: Tries (1 pt each)

Activities: Tries, Touches, Rollballs, Dummy Half Plays

Timer: 2 halves (20 minutes each)

⚽

Soccer (Football)

Scoring: Goals (1 pt each)

Activities: Goals, Assists, Shots, Saves, Corners, Fouls, Cards

Timer: 2 halves (45 minutes each)

🏑

Field Hockey

Scoring: Goals (1 pt each)

Activities: Goals, Assists, Penalty Corners, Saves, Cards

Timer: 4 quarters (15 minutes each)

🏐

Volleyball

Scoring: Rally scoring to 25 points, win by 2

Activities: Serves, Spikes, Blocks, Digs, Assists, Aces, Faults

🤽

Water Polo

Scoring: Goals (1 pt each)

Activities: Goals, Assists, Steals, Blocks, Exclusions

Timer: 4 periods (8 minutes each)

Racquet Sports

Precise point-by-point scoring for racquet sports with set/game tracking, serve monitoring, and advantage/deuce handling.

🎾

Tennis

Scoring: Points → Games → Sets (Best of 3 or 5)

Activities: Aces, Double Faults, Winners, Unforced Errors, Serve %

Special Features: Advantage/deuce tracking, serve/return stats, tiebreak support

🏸

Badminton

Scoring: Rally scoring to 21 points (Best of 3 sets)

Activities: Smashes, Drops, Clears, Net Shots, Service Errors

Special Features: Rally scoring rules

🎾

Squash

Scoring: Rally scoring to 11 points (Best of 5 sets)

Activities: Winners, Errors, Lets, Strokes

Special Features: Rally scoring, let/stroke tracking

🎾

Racquetball

Scoring: Rally scoring to 15 points (Best of 3 sets)

Activities: Aces, Errors, Kill Shots, Passing Shots

Special Features: Rally scoring rules

🏓

Pickleball

Scoring: Rally scoring to 11 points, win by 2 (Best of 3 sets)

Activities: Volleys, Dinks, Smashes, Service Errors

Special Features: Rally scoring, kitchen violations

Table Tennis (Ping Pong)

Activities: Serves, Returns, Winners, Errors, Spins
