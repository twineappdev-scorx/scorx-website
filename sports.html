Sports FAQ

How many sports does ScorX support?

ScorX currently supports 16 sports: 10 team sports (AFL, Basketball, Netball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Touch Rugby, Soccer, Field Hockey, Volleyball, Water Polo) and 6 racquet sports (Tennis, Badminton, Squash, Racquetball, Pickleball, Table Tennis).

Are sport-specific scoring rules built in?

Yes. Each sport uses its correct scoring system—AFL goals and behinds, basketball two and three-pointers, tennis sets and games with advantage/deuce, volleyball rally scoring, and more. No manual configuration is required to start scoring.

Can I track individual player statistics in team sports?

Yes. During live scoring you can attribute each action (goal, assist, tackle, rebound, etc.) to a specific player. After the game, full player-by-player breakdowns are available for all team sports.

What happens if my sport is not listed?

Email scorxapp@gmail.com to request a sport. We review all requests and add new sports in future updates. Sports that generate the most interest are prioritised for development.

Can timer settings be adjusted for my competition?

Yes. Period durations, number of periods, and timer display can all be configured to match your competition's specific rules—useful for modified junior formats that differ from standard rules.

Do racquet sports handle tiebreaks and advantage/deuce?

Yes. Tennis tracking includes full advantage/deuce handling, tiebreak support, and serve/return statistics. Badminton, Squash, Pickleball, and Table Tennis all use correct rally scoring rules with appropriate deuce/win-by-two handling.

Don't see your sport? We're always expanding.

Request a new sport at scorxapp@gmail.com and we'll consider it for future updates.
