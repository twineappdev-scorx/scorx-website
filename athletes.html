How to Build Your Individual Development Plan

Five steps in the Train tab, and the last one sends you back to the first. All of it is free.

  1. 1

    Measure where you are

    Free

    Log a baseline for speed, power, endurance and strength. There are 14 baseline metrics, plus one custom metric of your own. Save the results and you have a starting point you can point at, instead of a guess.

    Your age and sex benchmarks show where each number sits.

  2. 2

    Set a goal you can check

    Free

    Pick a metric and set a target for it. Goal mode draws a goal line on your progress chart, so you can see the gap and watch it close.

    Choose something you can work on this month, such as a faster 40m or a better 2km.

  3. 3

    Build your plan

    Free

    Your Development Plan holds one big goal, the skills you want to work on, an outcome goal and a strength and conditioning goal. Each skill gets two drills.

    The big goal is your BHAG: one goal for you as a player that the rest of the plan points at.

  4. 4

    Work the drills

    Free

    Open a skill to see its two drills, then put them into a Training Session. Save the drills you like as favourites. The Beep Test and the 800m and 2km time trials are free run drills too.

    Premium adds the full run drill library, with sprint and interval drills.

  5. 5

    Re-test and see the change

    Free

    After a few weeks of work, test again. Your chart shows the trend against your goal line. Hit it, and set the next one. Not there yet, and you know where to keep going.

    That loop is the whole plan.

Athlete Development FAQ

What is an individual development plan for a young athlete?

An individual development plan is a short, written answer to three questions: where you are now, what one or two things you are working on next, and how you will know it worked. It does not need to be formal. In ScorX you build it in the Development Plan in the Train tab: one big goal, the skills you want to work on with two drills each, an outcome goal and a strength and conditioning goal. You measure your starting point, set a target, and re-test to check it is working.

How do I set development goals that actually measure improvement?

Pick goals tied to actions you control and that get recorded every game, such as disposals under pressure, defensive efforts, shot accuracy or time on the ball, rather than goals tied to the scoreboard or selection. ScorX records those actions game by game, so a goal like "more contested possessions in the second half" can be checked against your activity timeline instead of debated in the car on the way home. For the physical side, goal mode lets you set a target for a metric and shows a goal line on your progress chart.

How does ScorX decide what I should work on next?

The Development Plan is yours to set, and it is free. You choose the skills and drills, and your recorded games and re-tests show whether it is working. ScorX Premium adds AI training recommendations, which read your game statistics, your own reflections and your athleticism metrics, then name a primary and secondary focus area with the evidence behind it. They update after 30 days or five new games, so a single bad game never rewrites your plan.

What athleticism metrics can I track as a young athlete?

There are 14 baseline athleticism metrics across speed, power, endurance and strength, plus one custom metric of your own on ScorX Free (Premium allows unlimited custom metrics). Recording and re-testing is free, and every metric has a trend and a progress chart so you can see your improvement. Age and sex benchmarking, which shows where you sit against age and sex averages, is also free.

How do Player Cards work?

Player Cards are generated from your game stats and profile photo, ready to share to Instagram, WhatsApp or the family group chat. One standard card style is free for everyone. ScorX Premium unlocks the full library, including classic, modern, sport-specific and sticker formats, plus custom colour themes and a full card configurator.

How long does it take to see progress?

Stats and highlights are there from your first game. Meaningful trends usually need about five games or a re-test of your athleticism metrics, which is roughly a month of a normal junior season. That is why ScorX Premium refreshes your AI training recommendations after 30 days or five games rather than after every match.

Do I need a coach to use ScorX?

No. You can record games, measure your athleticism and build a development plan on your own, or have a parent or coach tap in the scoring from the sideline. If your coach also uses ScorX, match invitations let stat recording be shared during games, and your history still belongs to you across clubs, teams and seasons.
