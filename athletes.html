Athletes FAQ

Can I use ScorX to track only my own stats as an individual athlete?

Yes. ScorX is built for individual athlete self-tracking. You can record your own performance in any game, build a complete career history, and receive AI training recommendations based on your personal statistics—no team or coach required.

What AI training recommendations does ScorX give athletes?

ScorX analyses your game statistics to identify your top skill development priorities. It generates personalised training session recommendations every 30 days or after 5 new games, targeting the specific areas where your performance data shows the greatest need for improvement.

What is Athleticism Benchmarking and how does it help me?

Athleticism Benchmarking lets you log your sprint speed, vertical jump, and endurance results, then compares them to age and gender-specific benchmarks. This tells you whether your development gaps are technical (skills), tactical (decision-making), or athletic (physical fitness), so your training is always focused on the right area.

How do I build a career performance portfolio?

Every game you score in ScorX is automatically saved to your career history. Family tier retains up to 2 years of game data with searchable archives, interactive performance charts, and season-by-season progression. This builds a comprehensive athletic portfolio you can export and share.

Can I track my performance across multiple sports?

Yes. ScorX supports 16 sports and you can switch between them freely. All your statistics are stored separately per sport, allowing you to monitor development across every discipline you compete in.

Is my performance data private?

Completely. ScorX is a privacy-first platform with no advertising, no third-party data sharing, and no analytics tracking of your personal data. iCloud sync uses Apple's end-to-end encryption. Your performance data belongs to you and can be exported at any time.
