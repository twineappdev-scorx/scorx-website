ScorX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 19 November 2026 | Effective Date: 19 November 2026

Quick Summary: No ads. We never sell your data. Data collection is opt-in and only used to improve the app. If you opt in to app analytics, we use a product analytics service (PostHog) to understand how the app is used. We don't use that data for advertising. Your game data is stored on your device and optionally synced via Apple iCloud (ScorX Premium).

Introduction

ScorX ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the ScorX mobile application. ScorX is operated by Twine App Dev, an Australian business, and we manage personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), including APP 1 (open and transparent management of personal information) and APP 5 (telling you what we collect and why).

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Account Information:

  • Apple ID (through iCloud authentication)
  • Subscription tier and payment information (processed by Apple)
  • Team names and configurations
  • Player names and statistics
  • Game data and scores

Optional Information:

  • Player photos (stored locally and optionally synced via iCloud)
  • Team logos and branding
  • Player contact information (emergency contacts, email, phone)
  • Game location data
  • Notes and comments

Automatically Collected Information

Device Information:

  • Device type and model
  • iOS version
  • App version
  • Device identifier (for sync purposes only)

Usage Data:

  • Features accessed within the app
  • Subscription tier usage metrics
  • Game creation and tracking statistics
  • Sync activity timestamps

Some of this usage data is collected through a third-party product analytics service. See "Product Analytics (PostHog)" below.

Product Analytics (PostHog)

The ScorX app uses PostHog, a product analytics service provided by PostHog Inc., to collect information about how the app is used. App analytics is opt-in. It is off until you choose to turn it on, and it is used only to improve the app.

What is collected if you opt in:

  • Product usage events, such as screens viewed and features used
  • Device and app information, such as device type, iOS version and app version
  • A pseudonymous account identifier. Analytics events are associated with your ScorX account, so they are personal information, not anonymous data. The identifier does not include your name or email address.
  • We never send the following to PostHog: player or team names, scores and statistics, photos, contact details (email address or phone number) or location.
  • Only crash and error reporting is switched on in PostHog. We do not use autocapture or session replay, and we do not store IP addresses or IP-based location (GeoIP).

Who processes it: PostHog Inc. processes this data on our behalf as a service provider, using PostHog's US cloud, so analytics data is stored and processed in the United States.

Why we collect it: To understand how the app is used, find and fix problems, and improve features and performance. We do not use analytics data for advertising, we do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it: Analytics data is kept for 30 days and then deleted. You can also ask us to delete your analytics data at any time.

How to opt in or withdraw: Analytics is off until you opt in. We offer the choice during onboarding, and you can switch it on or off at any time in Settings in the app. You can also contact scorxapp@gmail.com with questions or to ask us to delete analytics data associated with you.

Children: See "Children's Privacy" below for how analytics applies to younger users.

Our website: See "Our Website" below.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  1. Provide Core Functionality:
    • Store and manage your game data
    • Track player statistics and performance
    • Enable multi-device synchronization (ScorX Premium)
    • Process subscription purchases and manage access levels
  2. Improve User Experience:
    • Optimize app performance
    • Identify and fix bugs
    • Develop new features based on usage patterns, including analytics from PostHog
  3. Subscription Management:
    • Send trial expiration reminders (with your permission)
    • Notify about subscription renewals
    • Track usage against tier limits

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device, protected by your device's built-in security (passcode and iOS data protection)
  • Player photos and team logos are stored in app sandbox
  • Data persists even without internet connection

Cloud Storage (ScorX Premium)

  • Provider: Apple iCloud (CloudKit)
  • Container: iCloud.com.scorx.app
  • Sync: Your data syncs through your own iCloud account
  • Access: Only you can access your private CloudKit data
  • Retention: Data remains in CloudKit until you delete it or cancel subscription

Data Security Measures

  • Your data stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync
  • iOS Keychain for sensitive credentials
  • Secure API communication (HTTPS)
  • Apple's CloudKit infrastructure for iCloud sync
  • Analytics data is sent to PostHog over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)

Our Website

This section applies to visitors to our website (scorx.com.au), not to the app.

Our website does not use advertising pixels, third-party analytics or tracking cookies. We don't use the Meta Pixel or any similar tool. If we start using advertising or analytics tools on the website, we'll update this policy first, and those tools will only load if you agree to them.

A few services help deliver the website. They receive basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser contacts them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts.
  • YouTube plays the videos on our How it works page, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). A video loads from YouTube when the page loads, and YouTube may store information in your browser once you play it.
  • Buttondown runs our blog newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address goes to Buttondown to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

We use the following external services:

  • Apple CloudKit for cloud synchronisation (ScorX Premium). This is a first-party Apple service governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • PostHog Inc. for product analytics in the app, as described in "Product Analytics (PostHog)" above. PostHog processes analytics data on our behalf and only for the purposes described in this policy. PostHog is the only third-party SDK in the app. The app also uses Apple frameworks such as StoreKit and CloudKit.
  • We do not sell or rent your data
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not display third-party advertisements
  • We do not use Google Analytics or the Facebook SDK in the app

We may also disclose information where required by law.

Children's Privacy

ScorX can be used by children aged 4 and over with a parent or guardian's permission or management. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without that parental involvement.

Analytics and children: Many ScorX users are young athletes, and some are under 16. App analytics is opt-in and off until the person using the app opts in. The same opt-in is offered to everyone. There is no separate handling for users under 16 and no separate parent or guardian step. Parents and guardians can turn analytics off in Settings or contact us to ask for analytics data to be deleted. Analytics events do not include player or team names, scores, photos, contact details or location, and we do not use analytics data to advertise to anyone, including children.

Player profiles may include information about minors (team rosters), but this data:

  • Is controlled by the team manager/coach (adult user)
  • Is not shared with third parties
  • Remains private to the team
  • Can be deleted at any time

Parents/guardians can request deletion of minor player data by contacting scorxapp@gmail.com.

ScorX is available worldwide. Your data is stored:

  • CloudKit: In Apple's data centers based on your Apple ID region
  • Product analytics: PostHog Inc. processes analytics data in its US cloud, so analytics data is sent to the United States.
  • Compliance: We follow Apple's regional data protection requirements

Disclosure outside Australia (APP 8): When we disclose personal information to PostHog in the United States, we take reasonable steps to make sure PostHog handles it in a way that is consistent with the APPs.
