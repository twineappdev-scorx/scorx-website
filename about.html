Built in Brisbane.
Built for the Sideline.

ScorX started on a Saturday morning at a junior AFL ground in Kenmore, Queensland — and it hasn't left the sideline since.

Why ScorX Exists

ScorX was built by a solo developer based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. The idea came from watching junior AFL matches and noticing a consistent gap: parents were deeply invested in their child's athletic development, coaches were giving their time for free, but nobody had a simple way to capture what was actually happening on the field — let alone turn it into something useful for training the following week.

The apps that existed were either too complex for a grassroots coach running three teams on a weekend, too expensive for a family tracking one player, or built for elite programs that had dedicated analytics staff. Nobody was building for the Kenmore Bears. Nobody was building for the Saturday morning netball comp in Cairns or the after-school basketball clinic in Brisbane's west.

ScorX is that app. It's designed to be picked up by a parent who's never used a stats tool before, set up in under two minutes, and used every week without friction — while still producing insights that a serious coach or development-focused family can act on.

The Mission

Every junior athlete deserves a development story — a record of where they started, how they've grown, and what to work on next. That story shouldn't require a sports science degree to produce or a club budget to afford.

ScorX makes professional-grade athlete development intelligence accessible to Australian families and grassroots coaches — the people who show up every Saturday, drive to training twice a week, and care deeply about helping young athletes grow.

What ScorX Is

A youth sports performance tracking app for iOS

Available on iPhone and iPad running iOS 18.0 or later. Built natively for Apple platforms with full offline functionality — works anywhere, including remote grounds with no mobile signal.

Supporting 16 sports across Queensland and Australia

AFL, Basketball, Netball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Touch Rugby, Soccer, Field Hockey, Volleyball, Water Polo, Tennis, Badminton, Squash, Racquetball, Pickleball, and Table Tennis — each with sport-specific statistics and custom scoring rules.

Free for coaches. Premium for athlete development.

ScorX Free gives every coach unlimited teams, players, and games at no cost — always. ScorX Premium ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) adds the athlete development intelligence layer: AI training recommendations, athleticism benchmarking, Player Cards, Career Timeline, and season narratives.

Privacy-first, by design

No advertising. No third-party data sharing. No tracking of children's activity for commercial purposes. ScorX is GDPR and CCPA compliant. Your data lives on your device and syncs privately via iCloud.
